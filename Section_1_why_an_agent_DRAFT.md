## Section 1 - Why an Agent

Our health-insurance claim first-response system belongs on Rung 7 because the claim, rather than our code, determines the sequence and number of steps. A one-line claim may need only policy, procedure and hospital checks; a multi-line claim may trigger coverage checks and conditional pre-authorisation searches; a lapsed policy, exceeded annual limit or duplicate should terminate early. The paths therefore vary with the input and cannot be enumerated as one fixed workflow.

Rung 1, a single call, cannot obtain and verify the records needed for a defensible decision without preloading them and removing the loop. Rung 2, a prompt chain, has a fixed sequence and would either omit conditional evidence or waste calls on claims already requiring escalation. Rung 3 routing could send claims into lanes, but each lane would still contain variable line-item and pre-authorisation paths. Rung 4 parallelisation is useful: independent procedure, document and hospital checks can share a turn. It cannot replace the loop because a pre-authorisation lookup depends on learning that a procedure requires one. Rung 5 orchestrator-workers could allocate line checks dynamically, but delegated subtasks do not provide the observation-action loop needed to chase conditional evidence and gate a final write. Rung 6 evaluator-optimiser could improve a draft's wording or consistency, but critique cannot discover missing policy facts unless the system can choose and re-call tools. Only Rung 7 lets the model select its next retrieval from each observation and stop when the routing outcome is established. Its variable cost and latency therefore require a turn cap.

The two pre-build tests support, but also bound, this choice. Ground truth arrives within seconds from policy, procedure, pre-authorisation, hospital, document and decided-claim records, which can directly contradict the model. If those records were unavailable, delayed or subjective, we would retain a human-gated workflow. The first irreversible action is `issue_decision_letter`. Read-only looping before it is agentic retrieval; crossing this gate makes the system an agent, so the action requires confirmation, de-duplication and an evidence trail.

From D4 and D7, the measured run pass rate is **[P]** and the median turn count is **[T]**. The implied average per-step reliability is **s = P^(1/T) = [s]**. This is diagnostic, not causal, because steps differ and failures are not independent. Good performance means evidence-grounded routing, complete line-level dispositions, at most one gated action, explicit uncertainty instead of invention, and lower expected cost than manual handling.
